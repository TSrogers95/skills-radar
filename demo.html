A walk through Skills Radar

Five minutes on what it tracks, how the feed narrows to your provision, and what the levy forecast does with your own numbers.

The feed

Every change to funding rules, standards and T-Levels, with what changed, what it changed from, and what follows. Free to read without an account.

Your cohort

Choose the standards you deliver. The feed, the calendar and the compliance position all narrow to those, and nothing else.

Levy forecasting

Month by month on your own pay bill. It names the month co-investment starts, shows what expires unused, and prices cohorts you are only considering.

Try it yourself

The feed, the standards register and every article are free to read. A membership adds the personalised feed, the monthly newsletter and the levy forecast.
