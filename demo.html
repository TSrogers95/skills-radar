A walk through Skills Radar

What it tracks, how the feed narrows to the standards you deliver, and what the levy forecast does with your own numbers.

1

Start with the feed

Every change to funding rules, standards and T-Levels, with what changed, what it changed from, and what follows. Free to read without an account, and the quickest way to see whether this is useful to you.

See the feed
2

Then pick your standards

Choose what you deliver and everything narrows to those: the feed, the calendar, the compliance position. Out of hundreds of live changes you see the handful that touch your provision.

3

And forecast the levy

Month by month on your own pay bill and your own cohorts. It names the month co-investment starts, shows what expires unused, and prices apprentices you are only considering before you commit.

Try it yourself

Fourteen days of everything, free. Afterwards it drops to a smaller free account rather than closing, so nothing you set up is lost.
