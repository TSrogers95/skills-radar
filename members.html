Skills Radar Members

The public feed tells you what changed. Membership tells you what it means for you — filtered to the standards you actually deliver, with the dates in your own calendar and the funding modelled against your own payroll.

Your own compliance calendar

Twelve months of deadlines, filtered to the standards you deliver. Each date carries the action it requires and who it lands on, so preparation starts weeks ahead rather than the week of.

A feed built around your provision

Select every standard you deliver and the feed splits in two: changes to your programmes on one side, the funding rules that apply to everyone on the other.

Levy modelling against your payroll

Enter your pay bill and cohort. See annual funds in, what your apprentices commit you to, and the point you tip into 25% co-investment — before it lands on an invoice.

A newsletter that is actually yours

Weekly or monthly, built around your routes and standards. If something changes on a programme you run, it leads the email.
