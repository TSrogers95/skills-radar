What membership includes

A compliance calendar filtered to the standards you deliver, a feed showing changes that affect you, levy forecasting against your own figures, an off-the-job position against published minimums, and a board report you can print. The public parts of the site — the feed, articles, standards register and funding rules index — are free and always will be.

What it costs

, taken monthly by Stripe until you cancel. We will tell you at least 30 days before any price change, and you can cancel before it takes effect.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancel whenever you like, from the Your account tab in the members area. It takes a couple of clicks and you do not have to email anyone or give a reason. You keep access until the end of the month you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Your 14-day right to cancel. If you are buying as an individual rather than for a business, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of signing up and get your money back. That is the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and nothing here reduces it.

Because membership gives you access straight away, strictly we can charge for the part of the 14 days you have used. We do not. Cancel within 14 days and we refund the full £5, no questions and no deductions.

After 14 days. Cancelling stops the next payment. We do not automatically refund the month you are in, since you have had the use of it. But if you were charged for a month you did not mean to renew, email hello@skills-radar.co.uk and we will refund it. That is a policy, not a favour, and you do not need to explain yourself.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within five to ten working days depending on your bank. Ask for one at hello@skills-radar.co.uk and we will action it the same working day wherever we can.

If we get something wrong. If the site is unavailable for a long stretch, or you are charged in error, tell us and we will put it right — a refund, a credit, or whichever suits you better.

What this site is, and is not

Skills Radar summarises and indexes published information about apprenticeship funding, standards and T-Levels. It is a tool for finding and understanding what has changed. It is not legal, financial or compliance advice, and it does not replace the funding rules.

Every figure and every article links to its source, and where something is compiled automatically from a register rather than written by a person, the page says so. Before you act on anything here — particularly a deadline, a funding band, an off-the-job requirement or an eligibility rule — check it against the source we link to. Government guidance changes, sometimes without notice, and there will be periods when this site is behind it.

Levy and off-the-job figures are estimates based on what you enter. They are a planning aid, not a statement of your account. Your Apprenticeship Service account is the authority on what you actually hold and owe.
