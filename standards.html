Tap any figure above to filter the whole page, including the commonly delivered block. Grouped by the 15 Skills England occupational routes. Rows shaded cream have changed; rows shaded pink are losing funding. Where nothing has changed, the date of the last recorded change is shown so you know the entry is current rather than missing.

This is a tracked subset, not the full register. The Skills England register holds around 1,958 apprenticeships. Skills Radar tracks the standards most affected by current policy change plus a representative spread across every route. For the complete list, download the CSV from the Skills England apprenticeship finder or use the occupational maps public API.

Funding bands, durations and versions are recorded from the Skills England apprenticeship register at the review date. Always confirm against the register before contracting or costing a programme.
